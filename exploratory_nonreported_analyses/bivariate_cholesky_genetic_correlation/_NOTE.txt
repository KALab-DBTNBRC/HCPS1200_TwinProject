Bivariate Cholesky decomposition estimating genetic correlation (rA)
between paired structural and functional phenotypes. Reported in the
manuscript as explicitly exploratory, not a finding: "A bivariate
Cholesky model returned a high genetic correlation between reward-network
structure and function (rA = 0.98, 95% CI 0.92-1.00, p = 0.03) that did
not survive correction (q >= 0.13) and rests on a modest DZ sample."
Kept here, honestly labeled, same treatment as the other exploratory
folders -- not deleted, not presented as core evidence.
