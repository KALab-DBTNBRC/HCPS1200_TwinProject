The bi-tensor DIPY free-water-elimination (fwdti) approach was attempted
first and FAILED its own validation gate: the free-water-corrected MD was
nearly uncorrelated with standard MD (R^2~0.04), and the free-water
fraction f ran negative with age (real free water rises with age). The
single-voxel bi-tensor problem is ill-posed without spatial
regularization.

This was superseded by the NODDI/AMICO approach
(07_freewater_and_isotropic_modeling/01_noddi_isovf_extraction.py), which
passed its own age-direction validity gate and is the actual source of
the reported free-water exclusion in Table 4.

Kept here for transparency, not deleted -- same reasoning as the other
exploratory folders.
