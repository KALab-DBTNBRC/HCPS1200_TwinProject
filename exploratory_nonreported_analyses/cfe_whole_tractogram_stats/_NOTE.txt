Connectivity-based fixel enhancement (CFE) was applied to whole-tractogram
fixel statistics as an alternative to the a priori, four-network approach
used in the reported analysis. Results were non-significant and are not
the basis of any finding in the paper. Kept here for transparency rather
than deleted -- this is real analysis that was run, not a draft.

Depends on the tractography/SIFT2/CFE-matrix outputs of
05_diffusion_fba_pipeline/06_extract_metrics.py (Level 1) but is not
itself part of the reported pipeline.
